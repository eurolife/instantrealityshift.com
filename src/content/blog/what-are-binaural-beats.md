## The Healing Power of Sound: What Are Binaural Beats and Frequencies?

Sound has always had a mysterious, almost magical ability to move us. A single song can shift your mood, ease your mind, or even bring back long-lost memories. But did you know sound can also help relieve stress, improve sleep, and even reduce pain?

That’s where **binaural beats** and **healing frequencies** come in.

### What Are Binaural Beats?

Binaural beats are an audio illusion created when two slightly different frequencies are played—one in each ear. Your brain interprets the difference between them as a third “phantom” tone.

For example:
If one ear hears 200 Hz and the other hears 210 Hz, your brain “hears” a beat at 10 Hz.

This third frequency gently guides your brainwaves into different states—like relaxation, focus, or even sleep—depending on the frequency range.

Here’s a quick guide:

- **Delta (1–4 Hz)**: Deep sleep, healing, unconscious mind

- **Theta (4–8 Hz)**: Meditation, creativity, emotional release

- **Alpha (8–14 Hz)**: Calm focus, light relaxation

- **Beta (14–30 Hz)**: Alertness, active thinking

- **Gamma (30–100 Hz)**: High-level cognition, inspiration

### What Are Healing Frequencies?

Frequencies like 528 Hz, 432 Hz, and 396 Hz are often called “healing” or “solfeggio” tones. These pure tones are thought to promote emotional balance, physical healing, and spiritual alignment.

Each one resonates with different aspects of your energy:

- **528 Hz** – “The Love Frequency,” said to support DNA repair and heart-opening

- **432 Hz** – A natural tuning that feels harmonious and grounding

- **396 Hz** – Helpful for releasing guilt and fear

While scientific research is still growing in this area, many people find real comfort and transformation through regular listening.
